Welcome to Rehoboth Youth Mission Center, where faith inspires transformation and every young person has an opportunity to discover their God-given purpose. Through biblical discipleship, mentorship, leadership development, and community outreach, we are committed to raising a generation equipped to serve God, influence society, and bring lasting hope to their communities.

Our Vision

To raise a Christ-centered, spiritually mature and empowered generation of young people who transform their families, communities and future generations.

Our Mission

To reach, disciple, equip and empower young people through biblical teaching, evangelism, mentorship, leadership development, sports and practical life skills.

Our Scripture

1 Peter 2:9 — A chosen generation.

Reaching • Discipling • Equipping • Empowering

Our Programs

Youth Evangelism

Sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ with young people.

Youth Discipleship

Helping young people grow in their relationship with Jesus Christ through Bible study, prayer, mentorship, and spiritual guidance.

Mentorship and Life Skills

Guiding young people through biblical mentorship, character development, practical life skills, and personal growth to prepare them for a purposeful future.

Leadership Development

Raising Christ-centered young leaders through biblical leadership training, mentorship, responsibility, and opportunities to serve their communities.

Sports Outreach

Reaching young people through sports, building friendships, encouraging teamwork, and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.
