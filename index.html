Rehoboth Youth Mission Center

Raising a Generation of Faith, Hope and Purpose

Welcome to Rehoboth Youth Mission Center. We are committed to reaching, discipling, equipping and empowering young people through the love of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To raise a Christ-centered, spiritually mature and empowered generation of young people who transform their families, communities and future generations.

Our Mission

To reach, disciple, equip and empower young people through biblical teaching, evangelism, mentorship, leadership development, sports and practical life skills.
