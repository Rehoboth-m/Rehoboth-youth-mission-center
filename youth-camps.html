Kinja Youth Camp

Kinja Youth Camp is an annual Christ-centered gathering dedicated to helping young people grow in faith, discover their God-given purpose, and develop godly character.

Through biblical teaching, worship, prayer, mentorship, and fellowship, we equip young people to become strong disciples of Jesus Christ.

Kinja Youth Camp 2026

Dates: 7–11 December 2026

Location: Naivasha, Kenya

Theme: How to Hear God

Registration: KSh 1,000

Join us for five days of biblical teaching, worship, prayer, discipleship, fellowship, and life-transforming encounters with God.

Camp Activities

  • Biblical Teaching and Discipleship
  • Worship and Prayer Sessions
  • Personal Spiritual Development
  • Leadership and Mentorship
  • Sports and Team Building
  • Group Discussions and Fellowship
  • Evangelism and Outreach
