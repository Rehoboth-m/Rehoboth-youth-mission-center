About Rehoboth Youth Mission Center

Who We Are

Rehoboth Youth Mission Center is a Christ-centered ministry committed to reaching, discipling, equipping and empowering young people through the love of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To raise a Christ-centered, spiritually mature and empowered generation who will transform their families, communities and future generations.

Our Mission

To reach, disciple, equip and empower young people through biblical teaching, evangelism, mentorship, leadership development, sports and practical life skills.

Our Scripture

1 Peter 2:9 - A Chosen Generation
