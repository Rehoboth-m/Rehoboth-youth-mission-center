About Rehoboth Youth Mission Center

Who We Are

Rehoboth Youth Mission Center is a Christ-centered ministry committed to reaching, discipling, equipping and empowering young people through the love of Jesus Christ.

We believe every young person has God-given value, potential and purpose. Through discipleship, mentorship, leadership training and community outreach, we seek to help young people grow spiritually, develop their abilities and become positive influences in their families and communities.

Our Vision

To raise a Christ-centered, spiritually mature and empowered generation who will transform their families, communities and future generations.

Our Mission

To reach, disciple, equip and empower young people through biblical teaching, evangelism, mentorship, leadership development, sports and practical life skills.

Our Scripture

1 Peter 2:9 - A Chosen Generation

Pastor James Nyangoya Sitoki

Founder & Director

Rehoboth Youth Mission Center

Pastor James Nyangoya Sitoki is passionate about reaching young people with the Gospel of Jesus Christ, nurturing their spiritual growth, and equipping them to become responsible, Christ-centered leaders.

His ministry focuses on youth evangelism, biblical discipleship, mentorship, leadership development, youth camps, and community outreach.

Through Rehoboth Youth Mission Center, his vision is to raise a generation firmly grounded in God's Word, transformed by His love, and equipped to make a lasting difference in society.
